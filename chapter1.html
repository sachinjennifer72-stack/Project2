Chapter 1

The First Glance "You noticed me long before I noticed you."

It was our first day of college. I walked into the classroom wearing my lavender kurti, completely unaware that someone had already noticed me.

To me, it was just another normal day with new faces, a new classroom, and a little nervous excitement. But for you... it was something different. You saw the girl in the lavender kurti with open hair and big eyes, and somehow, that one little moment stayed with you. Meanwhile, I didn't even know you existed😭🤍 If someone had told me that the boy quietly looking at me that day would one day become my Dudu, I would've never believed them.

Funny how life works, isn't it?
One ordinary day...
One little glance...
And the beginning of our story❤️

📖 Chapter Note #1: Apparently, all it took was one look... and you had already decided I was your favorite person🤭❤️
