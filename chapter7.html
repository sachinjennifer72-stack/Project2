Chapter 7
Until We Meet Again 🚆🤍

"Every goodbye became easier... because I knew you'd be there for it."

Every time college closed for holidays, it meant one thing... I had to go back home. But before every journey, there was one thing I could always count on...

You🤍

You would come with me to the railway station, carry my bags, find my seat, make sure I was comfortable, and only then would you think about leaving. And just before the train started moving...

You'd gently kiss my forehead and say goodbye.

It was never a big moment. Just a quiet little ritual that made every goodbye a little less difficult. As the train slowly left the station, I'd look out of the window until I couldn't see you anymore... Already waiting for the day I'd come back.❤️ No matter how many times I left, thank you for making sure I never felt alone until the very last minute. See you soon, Dudu. 🤍🚆

📖 Chapter Lesson #7: Goodbyes hurt a little less when they're wrapped in a forehead kiss
