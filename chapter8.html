Chapter 8
The Pizza Surprise 🍕❤️

"I thought someone had delivered a pizza... instead, someone delivered the biggest surprise."

It was another college holiday, and I was back home in Bettiah. One afternoon, you called and casually said,

"Go open the gate... I've ordered pizza for you." 🍕

As I walked towards the gate, I was already complaining, "Why did you order pizza? There was no need!" The moment I opened the gate... I recognized you instantly. Even though your face was covered with a handkerchief, I knew it was you. I just stood there, completely speechless. You had travelled all the way from Patna to Bettiah... just to surprise me. Because my family was around, I couldn't even stand there and talk to you properly. I quietly took the pizza, looked at you for a few seconds, and before I knew it... You were already leaving. You rode all the way back to Patna in the scorching heat. Your scooty gave you trouble on the way, you were figuring out the route with Google Maps, and somehow reached home by evening. All that... Just to surprise me for a few precious moments.🤍 Next time, don't hide behind a handkerchief... I know my Dudu even from a distance.🤍🍕

📖 Chapter Lesson #8: "The best surprises aren't delivered to your door... they stand waiting outside it."❤️
