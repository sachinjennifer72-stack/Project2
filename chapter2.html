Chapter 2
The Chocolate Incident 🍫

"It all started because someone didn't share one chocolate..." 🤭

The day before, I told you I was hungry. Later, you participated in a game and won one chocolate. Obviously, I thought, "He's going to give it to me."😌

But nope... You gave it to someone else...😤😂

The next day, I made sure to remind you. "Wow... you won a chocolate and didn't even think of me?"🤨🍫 I kept teasing you, never expecting you to actually do anything about it. But after lunch, there you were... Standing in front of me with a plastic box full of chocolates. For a second, I just stared at you. "Wait... all of these are for me?" From expecting one chocolate... to getting a whole box... You really knew how to make up for it❤️ But I still think I deserved that first chocolate.

📖 Chapter Lesson #2: Never tease Dudu about chocolates... he might come back with a whole box. 😂🤍
