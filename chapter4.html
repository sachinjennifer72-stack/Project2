Chapter 4
The Jealous Scooty 🛵

"Apparently, even a scooty can make someone jealous." 🤭

Every time we rode together, I would hold the back handle of the scooty instead of you. Not because I didn't want to hold you... I was just too shy. One day, you finally complained,

"I'm jealous of the back of my scooty!"😂

Then you said,

"Does my shoulder have thorns or something? Why do you always hold the scooty instead of me?" 🤨

I couldn't stop laughing. Poor Dudu... Lost a competition to a scooty handle🤭❤️ Dear Scooty Handle, thanks for taking care of me back then... your duty is officially over now. 😂❤️

📖 Chapter Lesson #4: Every relationship has a third wheel... ours was the scooty handle🛵🤍
