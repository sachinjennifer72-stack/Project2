Chapter 6
The Days We Grew Together 🌸

"Some of the best memories were never planned... they simply happened because we were together." 🤍

Somewhere between lectures, assignments, and attendance...

We became a team❤️

We studied together on video calls, helped each other before exams, celebrated being the toppers, and somehow even ended up becoming Class Representatives together. 🤭 College events became more fun because you were there. Whether it was dancing, hosting, cheering each other on, or just sitting together in the audience, every event gave us another memory to smile about. Then came our little dates, movie plans, café visits, random selfies, and countless moments that never made it to our camera roll but stayed forever in our hearts. Looking back now... I don't remember every lecture we attended. But I remember every moment we spent together. 🤍

📖 Chapter Lesson #6: "The best college degree I earned... was finding my favorite person." 🎓🤍
