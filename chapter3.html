Chapter 3
The Excuse to Text Me 📱💙

"Sometimes, all someone needs is one little reason to start a conversation."

One day in class, you asked if I could send you my notes. Being me... I casually replied,

"I'll send them to Aditya. You can take them from him." 🤷🏻‍♀️😂

Little did I know... someone already had a completely different plan. Instead of riding your scooty home like every other day, you gave it to your friend and walked all the way with me and my roommates. Somewhere during that walk, you smiled and said,

"Give me your number... I'll take the notes from you directly." 🤭

Of course, I gave you my number. Later, I found out something that made me laugh even more... You already had my number from the class group. You just didn't want to message me out of nowhere because you thought it would look like you were stalking me. So all you needed... ...was one tiny excuse to text me. ❤️ That evening, our first chat turned into our first call... And a conversation that was supposed to be about notes somehow became the beginning of countless conversations. 📞🤍 Poor Dudu... you already had my number the whole time. All that planning just to send one message. 😂❤️

📖 Chapter Lesson #3: "Sometimes, 'I need your notes' actually means 'I just want to talk to you.' 🤭📚💙"
