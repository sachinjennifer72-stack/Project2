Chapter 9
Meet Our Little Family 🐧🤍

"Two plushies... four hearts... one tiny family"

After everything we had been through—the fights, the misunderstandings, the distance, and all the little storms—we were still here. One day, you surprised me with my favorite plushies. The moment I saw them, my heart melted. But they didn't stay just plushies for long... We proudly named them

Chotka and Motka

and just like that, we became their very serious (and slightly crazy) parents. 😂❤️ From then on, they weren't just toys. They became part of our little world—our inside jokes, our silly conversations, and our happiest moments. Maybe that's what love feels like. Creating a home, even if it starts with two fluffy penguins.

📖 Chapter Lesson #9: Home isn't always a place. Sometimes it's two people, two plushies, and a heart full of love🏡❤️
