Chapter 5
Food, Coffee & A Little Investment ☕🍗

"Some dates are remembered for the place. Ours is remembered for the food... and one unforgettable sentence."🤍

You had stopped eating non-veg for almost two years. Then one day, you looked at me and said,

"Let's have biryani."

I still don't know if you started eating it again because of me... but I secretly like to believe I had something to do with it. 🤭🍗 One day, you also took me to your favourite café. You made me try your favourite coffee and milkshake... And I proudly introduced you to lava cake, which somehow became one of our favourites after that. 🍫✨ While we were eating, I looked at you and asked,

"Why are you doing so much for me?"

You just smiled and said,

"It's all an investment on you... for our future." ❤️

I laughed... But a tiny part of my heart quietly whispered,

"I hope this investment lasts forever." 🤍

📖 Chapter Lesson #5: Never underestimate the power of biryani, coffee, and lava cake—they're dangerous for the heart😂🍗☕🍫
